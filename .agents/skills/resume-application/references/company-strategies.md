# Company strategy hints

These are prompts for discovery, not facts or mandatory fields. The actual site controls.

- Private companies often emphasize delivered outcomes, technical stack, availability, compensation, and concise role-specific descriptions.
- State-owned and central state-owned organizations may ask for political affiliation, party dates, household registration, archive location, graduate status, certificates, and detailed education. Fill only confirmed facts and leave unsupported declarations unresolved.
- Multinationals may ask for English name, English resume, language scores, work authorization, travel/relocation, and privacy/equal-opportunity declarations. Treat legal declarations and work authorization as high-risk confirmation fields.

The selected resume variant guides role-specific wording. It does not override the public facts profile and does not create an objective fact.
